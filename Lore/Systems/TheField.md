# The Field

---

## 1. One Field, Two Polarities

There is one field. It permeates all space. It is the same field that governs charge, heat, molecular bonds, and every physical interaction, and it is also the substrate that makes soul cores, fragments, identity, and consciousness possible. It does not become a different thing depending on how it expresses. It is one quantity with two polar orientations: a physical polarity and a spectral polarity.

These are not two separate forces coexisting in the same space. They are two expressions of the same conserved quantity. The field's natural state tends toward balance between both orientations. A persistent bias toward one polarity at any location means there is proportionally less expressing as the other, not because anything is displaced or consumed, but because they are the same thing oriented differently. More of one is necessarily less of the other.

---

## 2. The Physical Polarity

The physical polarity is the electromagnetic force in all its familiar expressions: charge separation, field geometry, molecular bonds, heat flow, and the electrochemical signalling of living nervous systems. Every physical interaction at every scale, from the subatomic to the macroscopic, is an expression of the field in its physical orientation.

This polarity is what [[MagicSystem|elemental casters]] work with. When a caster moves heat, builds a charge imbalance, or catalyses a molecular reaction, they are redirecting the field's physical-polarity expression. The force they manipulate is real, measurable, and consistent. It requires no belief in the spectral polarity to function, and in environments where the spectral fraction is negligible, the physical polarity is entirely sufficient to describe everything happening.

Any sustained source of electromagnetic activity, including machinery, infrastructure, and biological nervous systems, drives the local field balance toward physical expression. The field is always responding to what is present in a given location. A city full of EM output is, in field terms, a place that has been continuously pushed toward its physical orientation.

---

## 3. The Spectral Polarity

The spectral polarity is the orientation of the same field that underlies consciousness, identity, and the structures of the [[SoulSystem|soul system]]. Soul cores, field shells, and [[Fragments|fragments]] are all structures of the field's spectral polarity. They are not supernatural additions to physical reality. They are physical reality, oriented differently.

This polarity is what spectral practitioners work with. When a practitioner senses a soul core, reinforces a field shell, or disperses fragments from a location, they are engaging with the field in its spectral orientation, the same underlying field an elemental caster redirects approached from the other end.

The spectral polarity does not manifest as force, heat, or charge. Its expressions are structural: the organised coherence of a soul core, the boundary of influence that constitutes a field shell, the accumulated identity bias of a fragment mass. Measuring any of these with physical instruments is not possible; they are expressions of the field in the orientation those instruments are not designed to detect.

---

## 4. Why Neither Tradition Knows

Neither of the two traditions that work with these polarities knows they are studying the same thing.

Elemental casters in cities work with a powerful, consistent physical phenomenon and have no reliable access to spectral expressions. The environment that makes their practice work actively suppresses the fraction of the field that spectral practitioners use. They have no reliable evidence that the spectral polarity is real, and every reason to treat their work as a complete and self-contained physical discipline.

Tribal spectral practitioners work in environments where the spectral fraction is rich and the physical-polarity saturation of urban infrastructure is absent. They have no electromagnetic machinery to study and no framework for understanding what elemental casting is doing at the field level. Their tradition developed around the spectral orientation of a field whose physical orientation they do not manipulate.

Both are correct that the forces feel and behave differently; they are different expressions of the same quantity and have genuinely different phenomenology. Neither is correct that they are unrelated. A practitioner with deep fluency in both would understand something neither tradition currently does: that the body and the soul, physical casting and spectral practice, are two approaches to the same underlying phenomenon.

---

## 5. Local Field Balance

The ratio of physical to spectral expression is not uniform across all locations. It varies continuously based on what is present and what has happened there.

### Natural Equilibrium

In open wilderness and areas with little sustained habitation, the field sits close to its natural equilibrium. Both polarities are present in roughly comparable fractions. Nothing is continuously driving the balance in either direction, and the field returns slowly toward this state when left undisturbed.

### Spectral Elevation

In areas with a history of prolonged intense emotion, ritual practice, or violent death, the spectral expression may be locally elevated above equilibrium. This occurs because detached fragments accumulate in such places over time, and fragment accumulation is itself a spectral structure that skews the local balance. The effect is gradual, cumulative, and persistent; a location associated with significant events over generations can develop a spectral fraction noticeably above what the surrounding region holds.

### Urban Suppression

Dense population centres push the local field heavily toward physical expression through two simultaneous mechanisms. The sustained electromagnetic output of infrastructure and technology, including machinery, electrical systems, and heat sources, continuously drives the balance toward physical. At the same time, the large number of active soul cores in a city continuously draw on the field's spectral polarity to maintain their own structure, reducing the available spectral fraction further. The combined effect is an environment where the spectral polarity makes up only a small fraction of what is present, enough to exist but not enough to sense or manipulate reliably.

The field itself is always present in cities. It is the ratio of expression that dense habitation has driven out of reach.

### Practical Consequence

Because each tradition developed in an environment where the other polarity was suppressed, each developed its expertise in a context that actively hid the other half of the phenomenon. City civilisation has no reliable spectral phenomena to study and no cultural framework for interpreting them. Tribal communities lack the electromagnetic saturation that makes elemental casting powerful and have not developed tools for working with it. Each tradition's blindspot is the other's home territory, not because either is wrong, but because the field itself arranged the conditions that kept them separate.
