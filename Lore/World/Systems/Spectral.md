# The Spectral School

---

## Overview

Spectral practice is the deliberate engagement with the spectral pole of the underlying force: the substrate that produces soul cores, sustains field shells, generates and binds [fragments](Fragments.md), and underlies identity and consciousness. Where elemental casting works outward on matter and physical processes, spectral practice works inward on the structures of experience and identity.

Spectral practitioners do not manipulate the physical world directly. They work with the [soul system](SoulSystem.md): sensing its structures, reinforcing or disrupting its components, guiding its processes at critical transitions. Their work is largely invisible to physical examination and produces effects that mainstream civilization, lacking both the spectral force concentration to study these phenomena and the cultural framework to interpret them, consistently misattributes to psychology, mental illness, or coincidence.

All spectral work shares the same practical constraints as elemental casting. Energy comes from the environment or a [crystal](Crystals.md), not from the practitioner's own soul. Coherence determines control. Distance multiplies cost. Working with one's own soul energy directly is possible but degrades the soul core over time.

---

## Cross-Domain Training

Casters specialize in one primary domain. The specialization is not a hard physical law but a practical reality rooted in the depth of training required to work safely and effectively in any domain. Each domain requires deep specialist training, and fluency in a second domain represents an unusual and demanding life investment rather than a natural extension of existing practice.

## Domains

### Perception

Spectral perception is the foundational domain of the school. It is concerned entirely with sensing rather than manipulation: reading soul structures, detecting fragment mass and emotional content, identifying disembodied cores in an environment, and assessing the spectral state of a location or person.

Every other spectral domain depends on accurate perception. Shell work performed without reliable perception of the soul structure being worked on is as dangerous as surgery performed blind. Fragment work without the ability to sense fragment density, quality, and attachment is guesswork. Transition work without the ability to perceive the state of a disembodied core is intervention in a process the practitioner cannot actually observe.

Most practitioners in the spectral school develop baseline perception as part of their foundational training regardless of their eventual specialization. The domain becomes its own specialization when a practitioner focuses on developing perception to extraordinary sensitivity, achieving the ability to read subtle structures that trained practitioners in other domains cannot detect. A specialist perceiver is often the most valuable member of any group undertaking complex spectral work.

### Fragment Work

Fragment work is the most accessible domain in the spectral school and the most widely practiced across different cultures and traditions. It concerns [fragments](Fragments.md), secondary spectral structures that accumulate around the soul core over a lifetime, carrying emotional bias, identity tendency, and memory essence.

Practical applications include drawing loose environmental fragments into stable structures, dispersing accumulated fragments from a location, reading the emotional history carried in a person's fragment mass, reinforcing weakened fragment binding in someone who has suffered trauma or significant loss, and cleansing corrupted or misaligned fragments that are producing destabilizing influences on the host's experience and behavior.

Fragment work is the domain most likely to have a cultural presence even in communities with only partial understanding of the spectral force. Because fragments are the most abundant and observable spectral phenomenon and their effects on personality and emotional experience are pervasive, almost every culture that interacts with the spectral force at all has developed some form of fragment practice, even if it is framed entirely in spiritual, religious, or psychological terms rather than physical ones.

### Shell Work

Shell work concerns the field shell surrounding the soul core: the boundary of influence that maintains the coupling between soul and brain and holds the fragment mass in proximity to the core. A shell work practitioner can strengthen or weaken this coupling, reinforce a shell that has been damaged by trauma or illness, and intervene in possession events by working directly with the interaction between a resident core and an intruding one.

This is the mechanism underlying effective exorcism. A shell work practitioner does not destroy an intruding soul core. They work with the relative strength of the two cores and the state of the host's shell to either reinforce the resident's coupling sufficiently to resist the intrusion, or in cases where the resident core has already been displaced, to manage the transition in a way that minimizes damage to the host's neural architecture and fragment mass.

Shell work is rare and considered the most demanding spectral specialization because the consequences of imprecision are catastrophic. Working directly with the soul-brain coupling means operating adjacent to the physical structure of the self. An error does not produce a suboptimal outcome. It produces identity damage, permanent fragmentation, or in the worst cases complete dissolution of the resident soul's structural integrity.

### Transition Work

Transition work concerns the states surrounding death: the post-death persistence window, the process of a disembodied core's collapse, facilitation of volitional dissolution, and management of the rare circumstances where a persisting core is at risk of rebinding in a way that will cause harm.

A transition practitioner working with the recently dead is not communicating with the dead in any meaningful experiential sense. A disembodied core has no brain and therefore no experience. The practitioner is working with a physical structure in a particular state of decay, assessing its remaining stability, and either facilitating its clean collapse or in exceptional circumstances stabilizing it temporarily for a specific purpose.

This domain is the most saturated with cultural interpretation of any in the spectral school, because death is universally significant and every culture has developed practices around what physically happens at and after it. Two transition practitioners from entirely different cultural backgrounds may perform nearly identical physical interventions while describing them in completely irreconcilable terms. The physical reality underneath their practice is the same. The frameworks they use to understand and communicate it are not.

### Core Perception

Core perception is distinct from general spectral perception in that it concerns the soul core itself rather than the structures surrounding it. Where general perception works with the field shell and fragment mass, both of which are large enough to sense with trained but not exceptional sensitivity, core perception requires the ability to detect and read a structure that is infinitesimally small and shielded by the shell and fragment mass around it.

Practical applications include assessing the structural integrity of a soul core, sensing damage or unusual accumulation, evaluating whether a core is at risk of imminent collapse, and detecting the presence of an unusually dense or stable disembodied core in an environment. This last application is the primary tool used by practitioners who specialize in tracking [demonic entities](Demons.md).

Core perception is the rarest spectral specialization and the one least likely to have developed independently across multiple cultural traditions. The sensitivity required to practice it reliably takes years to develop even in spectrally rich environments, and most practitioners who attempt it plateau at a level of sensitivity sufficient for shell and fragment assessment but insufficient for reliable core reading. The few who develop genuine core perception are exceptional in a way that exceeds ordinary domain mastery.
