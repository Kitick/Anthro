# Elemental Conversion and Energy Domains

## Overview

Elemental abilities are not the manipulation of matter itself, but the conversion of ambient field energy into specific physical processes. Casters do not create energy; they align, draw, and redirect energy that already exists in the environment, using crystals as buffers and stabilizers. The visible “elements” are emergent effects of how that energy is released into the world.

All elemental work follows three universal constraints:

- **Energy must come from the environment or a crystal.** Using one’s own soul energy is possible but dangerous and shortens lifespan.
- **Alignment determines control.** The caster must maintain physical, emotional, and cognitive coherence. Misalignment causes instability, energy dumping, or uncontrolled effects.
- **Distance multiplies cost.** Energy is easiest to shape near the body or through physical contact. Remote effects require extended alignment, environmental paths, or anchored crystals.

## Crystals and Energy Handling

Crystals act as magnets and reservoirs for field energy. Their crystalline lattice naturally attracts and traps energy from the surrounding environment, slowly charging over time. When used by a trained caster, a crystal serves three functions:

- **Buffer:** Stores energy so the caster does not draw directly from themselves.
- **Amplifier:** Allows greater output than ambient energy alone would permit.
- **Anchor:** Enables remote casting by creating a secondary alignment point.

Crystals have limited input and output rates. Overuse causes heat buildup, light emission, structural fatigue, or fracture. A crystal that is destroyed while active releases its stored energy violently.

## Energy Conversion Domains

Casters specialize in one primary domain. Each domain represents a different way energy can be safely aligned into the world. Attempting to cross domains without training results in rapid instability.

### 1. Electrical Domain (Charge and Potential)

Electrical casters create and release charge imbalances. Lightning is not created, but occurs when stored potential discharges through a conductive path.

**Typical effects**

- Directed electrical arcs between conductive points
- Disruption of neural signaling (paralysis, numbness)
- Interference with machines and sensors

**Risks**

- Back-arcing into the caster
- Nervous system damage
- Crystal discharge cascades

### 2. Thermal Domain (Heat Flow)

Thermal casters control temperature gradients, moving heat from one place to another. Fire is a secondary effect of rapid heating, not the goal itself.

**Typical effects**

- Ignition, melting, or cauterization
- Freezing by heat extraction
- Thermal stabilization to prevent shock or injury

**Risks**

- Severe burns or frostbite
- Structural weakening
- Heat feedback through the crystal

### 3. Kinetic Domain (Motion and Pressure)

Kinetic casters create pressure differentials that cause movement in gases, liquids, or solids. Air and water manipulation are both expressions of this domain.

**Typical effects**

- Pushing, pulling, or lifting objects
- Shockwaves and concussive blasts
- Vibration control (shattering, dampening, sound shaping)

**Risks**

- Loss of control with distance
- Structural collapse
- Internal strain and joint damage

### 4. Chemical Domain (Reaction Control)

Chemical casters alter reaction rates and pathways in matter. Their work is blind to structure and therefore extremely dangerous in living systems.

**Typical effects**

- Accelerated decay, corrosion, or rusting
- Neutralization of acids, toxins, or explosives
- Catalytic ignition or explosive reactions

**Risks**

- Uncontrolled chain reactions
- Environmental contamination
- Severe injury if used near life

### 5. Biological Domain (Living Organization)

Biological casters guide existing biological processes, providing energy and regulation to living systems without rewriting them. They cannot act on dead tissue or nonliving matter.

**Typical effects**

- Accelerated wound healing and tissue repair
- Stabilization of shock, pain, or organ failure
- Metabolic reinforcement for endurance or recovery

**Risks**

- Extreme exhaustion
- Misguided growth (scarring, deformity)
- Long-term cellular strain
