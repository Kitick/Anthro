# Departments

All civil governance below Senate-level is handled through departments. Each department is autonomous within its domain, maintains its own internal structure and personnel ranks, and interfaces with the Senate through an assigned Senate Liaison. The Senate sets broad policy and approves major resource or regulatory decisions; departments handle all execution within those parameters.

The military operates as its own department but is structurally separate from civil departments. See [Military.md](Military.md).

---

## Department of Energy

Manages the nation's power infrastructure, including generation, distribution, and long-term capacity planning.

**Senate controls:** Energy cost ceilings, prioritization of energy types, approval of land acquisition for new facilities, and major budget allocations.

**Department handles:** Plant construction and maintenance, grid operations, staffing and training, technical safety standards, regulatory enforcement within the energy sector, and reporting to the Senate.

**Structure:** Led by a Director General with division heads overseeing generation, distribution, safety, and planning. Each division operates with its own technical staff and regional leads.

---

## Department of Transportation

Manages the nation's movement infrastructure: roads, rail, transit systems, ports, and air corridors.

**Senate controls:** National transit priorities, funding allocation for major corridor projects, approval of new infrastructure that crosses jurisdictional or regional boundaries.

**Department handles:** Construction and maintenance of transit infrastructure, traffic and logistics regulation, licensing and safety standards for carriers, and coordination with city-level transit systems.

**Structure:** Led by a Director General with divisions for ground, rail, air, and maritime. Regional administrators manage infrastructure within defined geographic zones.

---

## Department of Health

Manages public health systems at the national level, including hospitals, disease response, medical standards, and health policy.

**Senate controls:** National health priorities, funding for major public health initiatives, standards for medical licensing.

**Department handles:** Oversight of public medical institutions, national disease monitoring and response, medical licensing and standards enforcement, mental health policy, and research grants for applied medical work.

**Structure:** Led by a Director General with divisions for physical health, mental health, epidemiology, and medical standards. City-level hospitals and clinics operate semi-independently but answer to departmental standards.

## Department of Communications

Manages national information infrastructure: broadcast networks, communications systems, information standards, and media regulation.

**Senate controls:** National communications priorities, licensing of major broadcast networks, approval of infrastructure that affects national security or sovereignty.

**Department handles:** Maintenance and regulation of communications infrastructure, broadcast licensing below Senate level, standards for public information systems, and signal coordination.

**Structure:** Led by a Director General with divisions for infrastructure, broadcast regulation, and public information. Operates technical facilities distributed across major population centers.

---

## Department of Infrastructure

Manages civil construction and maintenance that does not fall under a specialized department: public buildings, utilities, water systems, and urban planning at the national scale.

**Senate controls:** Approval for major land acquisition, national building standards, large-scale urban development priorities.

**Department handles:** Construction and upkeep of national civil infrastructure, water and sanitation systems, coordination with city governments on large urban projects, and contractor oversight.

**Structure:** Led by a Director General with divisions for civil construction, utilities, urban coordination, and environmental compliance. Works closely with Energy and Transportation on shared infrastructure.

---

## Department of Justice

Manages the national legal system, including courts, legal standards, and federal-level law enforcement. City-level law enforcement (police, fire, emergency response) operates independently under city governance and is not a federal department function.

**Senate controls:** Broad legal standards and boundaries that courts must operate within, approval of major judicial appointments at the national level, and oversight of the impeachment process.

**Department handles:** Operation of national courts, adjudication of cross-jurisdictional disputes, federal investigation bodies for crimes that cross city or regional lines, maintenance of national legal code within Senate-defined bounds, and enforcement of departmental regulatory violations that require legal action.

**Structure:** Led by a Director General with divisions for national courts, federal investigation, legal standards, and regulatory enforcement. National courts operate at multiple levels, from regional courts up to a national appellate body.
