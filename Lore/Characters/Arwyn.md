# Arywn Lynne

**Species:** Caracal\
**Variant:** N/A\
**Gender:** Female\
**Age:** 22\
**Height:** 167 cm\
**Weight:** 59 kg\
**Role:** Herbalist

## Overview

Arwyn has lived her entire life in a rural village, far away from the troubles and technologies of the central cities. She has trained since childhood to become a healer and herbalist, and she is very driven to learn all that she can about medicine. As her training has always been her passion, rare plants and new medicinal recipes are incredibly exciting for Arwyn. She enjoys helping others, and is very emotionally intuitive by nature. She cares very deeply about her friends, and is a fierce defender of both her loved ones and her beliefs (spriritual as well as moral)

Arwyn's village gets raided by the military early in act 1, and this forces her to leave her village for the first time in her life. When she was 7 years old, her childhood best friend was possesed by a demon, and she witnessed a brutal exorcism. Due to this experience, she believes that all demons are evil beings that need to be dealt with accordingly. After leaving her village, she is persued for her healing abilities, and then decides to travel to the city in order to lose her persuers in the masses/crowds. Her personal objective becomes investigating the demons, and finding out more about spirituality.
