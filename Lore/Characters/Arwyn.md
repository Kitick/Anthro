# Arywn Lynne

**Species:** Caracal\
**Variant:** N/A\
**Gender:** Female\
**Age:** 22\
**Height:** 167 cm\
**Weight:** 59 kg\
**Role:** Herbalist

## Overview

Arwyn is an initiate apothecary in the clergy of Rivane, the city of rivers. The Patron Saint of Rivane is an advocate of inner reflection and self-awareness. Various meditation practices are a common part of Arwyn's daily life, as well as tending to shrines around the city burning herbs and incense. Rivane being such a lavish and appearence focused city, Arwyn has always felt like she is a bit different. She has always been more focused on her studies and work as a healer than outer beauty. She was never interested in the fashion or makeup that was in vogue among the other children her age growing up. Instead, she would lock herself away with books on herbal remedies or energy cleansing. 

During her childhood, Arwyn did manage to make one friend. This friend was also uninterested in trends and fashion, so they bonded over a shared sense of being outsiders. They each had a specific dream they were working towards, and supported each other in achieving their dreams. Her friend's dream was to travel and see countries outside of Senara, and to become a famous chef/baker?. This friend ends up being possesed by a demon, and the church sees fit to perform an exorcism to remove the evil spirit. After a brutal struggle, Arwyn's friend ends up dying. She has always blamed herself for not being a skilled enough healer to save her friend. After that day, Arwyn developed a deep hatred for demons, and began looking into more effective methods of exorcisms for evil spirits. She also started to question the practices of the church, as in her eyes they seemed harsh and ineffective.
