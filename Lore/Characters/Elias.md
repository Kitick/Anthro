# Elias "Ghost" Black

**Species:** Timber Wolf\
**Gender:** Male\
**Age:** 38\
**Height:** 185 cm\
**Weight:** 90 kg\
**Rank:** Ex Captain\
**Specialist:** Spec-ops

## Overview

Elias, who earned the nickname 'ghost' through his particular skill in stealth operations, is an ex-captain of the [military](../Locations/Praxis/Military.md) special operations. He is a practiced sharpshooter, with a proficiency in most firearms and martial weapons. His current weapons of choice are a longsword, and an easily concealable sidearm for emergencies (often fitted with a suppressor). Ghost's near perfect mission record earned him fear and respect throughout the military faction. He unexpectedly defected from the service after the disappearence of his mentor around 533 AP. After this event, he was actively pursued by the military faction. To this day, every unit who has gone after Ghost hasn't been able to capture him. While he can come off as brooding and intimidating, when he finds people he can trust implicitly he is unwaveringly loyal to his friends.

## The Mercenary Years

Relentlessly pursued across Praxis for his defection, Elias eventually fled to Senara where the military faction's reach weakened. The region offered anonymity, far enough from Praxis authorities that he could operate without constantly looking over his shoulder. For the past five years, Elias has drifted through Senara taking contracts as a freelance mercenary. This work pays well but keeps him perpetually untethered from others. He avoids powerful factions and long-term entanglements, moving from job to job with calculated detachment. The transience suits him; it makes him harder to find. Still, the years of isolation have worn on him in ways he doesn't often acknowledge. When a recent assignment damaged his longsword beyond his own ability to repair, he found himself traveling the rural roads in search of a skilled blacksmith—a mundane task that would lead him to Cindral and unexpectedly test the walls he's built around himself.
