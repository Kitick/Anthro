# Elias "Ghost" Black

**Species:** Timber Wolf\
**Age:** 34\
**Height:** 185 cm\
**Weight:** 90 kg\
**Rank:** Ex Captain\
**Specialist:** Spec-ops

## Overview

Elias, who earned the nickname 'ghost' through his particular skill in stealth operations, is an ex-captain of the military special operations. He is a practiced sharpshooter, with a proficiency in most firearms and martial weapons. His current weapons of choice are a longsword, and an easily concealable sidearm for emergencies (often fitted with a suppressor). Ghost's near perfect mission record earned him fear and respect throughout the military faction. He unexpectedly defected from the service after the disappearence of his mentor around (year) After this event, he was actively pursued by the military faction. To this day, every unit who has gone after Ghost hasn't come back alive. While he can come off as brooding and intimidating, when he finds people he can trust implicitly he is unwaveringly loyal to his friends.
