# Edmund Voss

## Overview

Edmund Voss is the Chancellor of [[Vesper]], a stern and disciplined nobleman whose public image is that of a practical ruler with little patience for sentiment. Five years ago, his daughter Seraphine "Sera" Voss, who traveled to [[Cindral]] for a research expedition, vanished without a trace. He never learns the full truth of what happened to her, but the disappearance left him restless, suspicious, and increasingly driven by a private obsession.

Although he does not know the details, Sera had befriended a young woman from [[Cindral]]. That friend was selected for the city's infamous "ascension ritual," a cruel sacrifice disguised as sacred tradition. When Sera tried to uncover the truth behind the ritual and planned to bring proof to the Vatican, she was silenced by the church's agents. Her father only knows that she disappeared during the trip and that the trail to [[Cindral]] went cold.

In the years since, Edmund has quietly devoted himself to uncovering the cult behind the ritual. He has no hard evidence to bring to the church hierarchy, so he refuses to make his accusations publicly. Instead, he wages a campaign of covert retaliation: hiring mercenaries, attacking merchant caravans bound for Cindral, sabotaging supply routes, and funding small acts of harassment designed to disrupt the cult's operations. His obsession has made him dangerous, and his methods have begun to blur the line between vengeance and a personal crusade.


## Personality

Edmund is careful, controlled, and deeply private. He speaks in measured tones, but beneath that composure lies a simmering fury and a ruthless streak. He is not cruel by nature, but he has become willing to do terrible things for the sake of destroying what he believes is a corrupt and monstrous system. His determination is unwavering, but it is increasingly fueled by grief and obsession rather than reason.

## Character History

Sera, whom he loved fiercely, had always been curious, intelligent, and unusually brave. Her disappearance shattered his faith in the safety and order of the wider world. Rather than accept the official explanations, he began gathering his own information, quietly tracing merchants, clergy, and travelers that could have crossed paths with her. That investigation eventually led him to the same terrifying conclusion that she had reached: the church of Cindral is hiding something profoundly evil.

Five years later, Edmund has not found the proof he needs to expose the truth publicly, so he remains in a precarious state of half-knowledge. He is determined to dismantle the cult's power through pressure and intimidation, even if that means operating outside the law. 

## Daughter

### Seraphine "Sera" Voss

- Species: European Badger
- Gender: Female
- Age: 24
- Role: Scholar / Researcher
- Personality: Curious, compassionate, and quietly stubborn. Sera was the sort of person who could not leave a mystery alone, especially if it involved people who were being harmed.
- Backstory: She traveled to [[Cindral]] to study the city's religious and civic traditions, hoping to document the practices of the church with objective curiosity. During her stay, she befriended a local girl who was later chosen for the ascension ritual. Sera began asking too many questions after realizing the ritual was not a blessing at all.
- Fate: She disappeared shortly after uncovering evidence of the cult's actions. Her father never learned the truth of what she found, only that she vanished.

## Friend from Cindral

### Mira Solen

- Species: Rabbit
- Gender: Female
- Age: 29
- Role: Cindral Resident / Ritual Candidate
- Personality: Warm, earnest, and deeply loyal to her friends. Mira was the kind of person who believed in the good intentions of her community, even as the truth around her became darker.
- Backstory: Mira became close friends with Sera during the latter's stay in Cindral. When the church announced her selection for the ascension ritual, she became a symbol of the system's brutality.
