# Edmund Voss

## Overview

Edmund Voss is the lord of [[Vesper]]. He becomes involved in [[Elias]]'s search for the bandit camp near [[Cindral]], eventually revealing the truth behind Cindral's "ascension ritual" — that it is a cultish sacrifice rather than the blessing the church's propaganda claims it to be.
