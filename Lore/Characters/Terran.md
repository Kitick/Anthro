# Terran Addari

**Species:** Cheetah
**Variant:** King
**Age:** 30
**Height:** 180 cm
**Weight:** 85 kg
**Rank:** Lieutenant
**Specialist:** Spec-ops

## Description

Terran is confident, disciplined, and socially adept within his unit, using humor and approachability to build cohesion and morale. In professional settings outside his squad, he is formal, controlled, and intolerant of inefficiency, maintaining clear boundaries between personal and operational relationships. He is highly trained, tactically intelligent, and capable of making rapid decisions under stress, which has accelerated his advancement through the ranks. Terran’s behavior is strongly shaped by expectations tied to his family’s military history, and he measures his worth primarily through performance and reliability. He consistently prioritizes mission success and the safety of his personnel over personal considerations, often suppressing his own needs to maintain command effectiveness.
