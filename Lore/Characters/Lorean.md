# Lorean "Noodle"

**Species:** Cinnamon Ferret\
**Gender:** Male\
**Age:** 28\
**Height:** 145 cm\
**Weight:** 50 kg

## Overview
From the cult city of [Cindral](../Locations/Senara/Cities/Cindral.md), Lorean lives as the governor's clerk. As one of the few officials who isn't part of the church heirarchy, most administrative tasks like paperwork and official filings fall to him and he is extremely overworked. It doesn't help that the governor is famously dismissive of regulatory tasks.

At 28, Lorean exists within Cindral's ascension window (ages 28-32)—the years during which the most spiritually pure citizens are selected annually for the ascension ritual, considered the highest honor in Cindral's faith. This proximity creates undercurrents of both aspiration and anxiety. His meticulous record-keeping and disciplined nature suggest spiritual devotion, yet his very attachment to worldly administrative duties contradicts Cindral's doctrine that material concerns and identity cloud the soul. He must grapple with the contradiction: is his role making him unworthy of ascension, or is his dedication to order and duty a reflection of true spiritual discipline?

The propaganda of Cindral's church frames ascension as transcendence—a blessing for those spiritually evolved enough. Lorean has seemed to accept this narrative, though his friendships and moments of levity (reflected in his "Noodle" nickname) suggest depths beyond simple compliance.

## Encounter with Elias
When Lorean encounters the mercenary Elias, something shifts. Elias carries stories from beyond Cindral's isolated borders—tales of different cities, different ways of living, different values. For the first time, Lorean is confronted with the reality that his worldview is not universal. Elias represents everything Cindral teaches him to reject: material concerns, worldly ambition, skepticism of religious authority. Yet Elias is also capable, thoughtful, and alive in ways that contrast sharply with Cindral's austere spirituality. 

It is only when Elias witnesses the ritual's true nature that the terrible reality becomes clear. In that moment of revelation and horror, Elias realizes what "ascension" truly means. He storms the ceremony to pull Lorean from the ritual's grip, forcing the young clerk into a choice he had never truly considered: flee with the outsider, or surrender to the "blessing" he had been raised to accept.
