# Kitick "Kit" Fox

## Overview

Kitick, called Kit by almost everyone who knows him, grew up in [[Maelis]]. His element is [[ElectroMagnetic|electrical]] and his mind is sharp—exactly what his city valued, but Maelis measured worth not just through intellect, but through proving oneself in strategic combat, through out-thinking opponents in the arena. He had no interest in the [[Tournament|monthly tournaments]] or the clash of wills that defined his city's culture. His father was a con artist and his mother was part of a fringe [[Interpretations|spectral]] movement he found intellectually dishonest. He had few friends. The most important one was [[Rathick|Rath]], who was everything the city valued and liked Kit anyway.

When military recruiters came to Maelis to attend the monthly tournament, Rath participated and made it to the semi-finals—exactly what Maelis culture expected of him, and exactly the kind of martial excellence the recruiters sought. Kit also entered the tournament, but was unremarkable; his tactical thinking and quickness of mind didn't translate into the kind of tournament success that caught attention. When a recruiter approached Rath with a formal offer of enlistment, Rath refused to accept without Kit. The recruiters were unmoved until Rath pressed the point, advocating insistently that Kit was a quick thinker, an agile problem-solver, someone whose adaptive mind was worth more than a dozen warriors. Reluctantly, he negotiated Kit's inclusion as a condition of his own service.

Enlisting was difficult for Kit. He was smaller than average and struggled through the physical intake program, nearly washing out entirely. A conversation in passing next to a grounded fighter jet changed his path. Given a few hours with an avionics problem that had stumped the maintenance team for days, he solved it. He was assigned to avionics, then software, then flight control development. His electrical sensitivity proved invaluable with the complex modern electronics in [[Praxis]]'s fleet, and Kit discovered he had aptitude and genuine interest. The work suited him in ways nothing in Maelis ever had. He proved himself slowly, through being right more often than was easy to dismiss.

He eventually earned enough institutional trust to get his space plane project approved—an experimental space-capable craft he had been designing on his own time, the first thing that had ever been entirely his. To build it properly, he quietly embezzled resources from the [[Military|military]] for years: budget gaps, unclear oversight, technical complexity used as cover. He justified it as correcting the institution's failure to properly fund a good idea. He was also, without fully acknowledging it, building something he could take with him. When he was discovered, he stole the plane and escaped—successfully, but not cleanly. The craft took damage during the escape and is in need of repair, grounded until he can get to it. Kit went to ground in the aftermath, moving constantly, living in survival mode. His injuries from the escape were severe enough that he needed medical care he couldn't safely seek. Weak, desperate, and running out of options, he was alone in the woods of [[Senara]] when the presence found him.

He had no framework for what it was. His home city of Maelis treated [[Demons|demons]] as folklore—distant enough that the word was more myth than concept. His best guess was something like that. His mother's spectral community might have known more, but he had dismissed everything they believed long ago. The presence was simply there, and the binding happened the way these things do—circumstance, proximity, a weakened barrier. The experience felt like conversations happening in the spaces between thoughts, articulate and patient. He couldn't name it; *spirit* didn't fit, *hey you* fit even less. What he understood was the offer: coexistence. He agreed with the pragmatism of someone in survival mode, facing death and given an unexpected alternative, without fully grasping the permanence of what he was accepting.

He doesn't learn what it actually is—that it is [[Pantheon|Aethos]], God of the Mind—until later. [[Arwyn]] carries a cultural framework he never had, and it's through that context that he works it out himself. By then, the change in him is already visible. His reserved personality is still there, but the demeanor of someone who had always been underestimated is finally set aside. Aethos, with full access to the way Kit's mind moved through the world, came to find that perspective worth staying for—and Kit, in turn, found the arrangement worth maintaining. The cooperation is mutual, arrived at rather than assumed. He is more direct now, more willing to pursue what he wants through the front door rather than the margins. He is also, in a way he notices and doesn't entirely like, not unlike the father he spent his life trying not to become. The fox irony is not lost on him.

He has a dry, almost imperceptible sense of humor that most people miss entirely. Rath always catches it.

## Relationships

**[[Rathick|Rath]]:** The oldest relationship he has. Rath is the one person Kit doesn't need to explain himself to, and doesn't. They have a shared language built over years. Kit asks a question at the right moment and Rath stops. Rath is the only person who reliably catches Kit's humor. After the binding, Rath is also the one most unsettled by who Kit is becoming, and the one whose reaction Kit actually cares about.
