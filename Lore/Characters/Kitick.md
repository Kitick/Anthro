# Kitick "Kit" Fox

**Species:** Silver Fox\
**Age:** 24\
**Height:** 170 cm\
**Weight:** 69 kg\
**Rank:** Sergeant\
**Specialist:** Pilot, Computer Systems

## Overview

Kitick, commonly called Kit, is reserved, quiet, and socially withdrawn, particularly in unfamiliar cultural environments. He processes information internally and speaks only when he is confident in his conclusions, which can make him appear distant or disengaged. Raised in an isolated tribal community alongside Rath, Kit was recruited into the military at a young age due to his exceptional aptitude for systems thinking, pattern recognition, and deductive reasoning. His technical abilities quickly earned him assignment to a restricted R&D hangar, where he works with engineering teams to develop advanced aircraft control systems—including experimental space-capable craft.
