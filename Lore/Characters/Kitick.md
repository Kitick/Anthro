# Kitick "Kit" Fox

**Species:** Silver Fox\
**Age:** 24\
**Height:** 170 cm\
**Weight:** 69 kg\
**Rank:** Sergeant\
**Specialist:** Pilot, Computer Systems

## Overview

Kitick, commonly called Kit, is reserved, quiet, and socially withdrawn, particularly in unfamiliar cultural environments. He processes information internally and speaks only when he is confident in his conclusions, which can make him appear distant or disengaged. Kit has a strong aptitude for systems thinking, pattern recognition, and deductive reasoning, and consistently outperforms peers in technical problem-solving tasks. This led to his assignment in a restricted R&D hangar, where he works with engineering teams to develop *advanced aircraft control systems*. He prefers structured, predictable environments and interacts more easily with machines than people, maintaining a low profile while remaining mission-critical through his technical expertise.
