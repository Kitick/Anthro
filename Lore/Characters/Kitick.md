# Kitick "Kit"

**Species:** Silver Fox\
**Gender:** Male\
**Age:** 24\
**Height:** 170 cm\
**Weight:** 69 kg\
**Rank:** Sergeant\
**Specialist:** Pilot, Avionics, Flight Control Systems\
**Element:** Electrical

## Overview

Kitick, called Kit by almost everyone who knows him, grew up in a small tribal community as the wrong kind of person in the wrong kind of place. His element was [electrical](../Systems/ElectroMagnetic.md). It was useful for reading power systems and circuit behavior, but meaningless in a tribe that measured worth through physical combat and elemental force. His father was a con artist and his mother was part of a fringe [spectral](../Systems/Interpretations.md) movement he found intellectually dishonest. He had few friends. The most important one was [Rath](Rathick.md), who was everything the tribe valued and liked Kit anyway.

When their community gradually fell apart and most of its members drifted into mainstream society, Rath stayed with Kit rather than leave him to figure it out alone. They settled in a nearby town, learned city life properly, and Kit spent years building a self-taught understanding of electronics, aviation systems, and power engineering through the city's technology networks. When a military recruiter approached Rath after witnessing him intervene in a street robbery, Rath agreed to enlist only on the condition that Kit came with him. Kit failed the physical intake program and nearly washed out entirely. A conversation in passing next to a grounded fighter jet changed his path. Given a few hours with an avionics problem that had stumped the maintenance team for days, he solved it. He was assigned to avionics, then software, then flight control development. His electrical ability let him feel circuit behavior the way others read schematics. He proved himself slowly, through being right more often than was easy to dismiss.

He eventually earned enough institutional trust to get his space plane project approved, an experimental space-capable craft he had been designing on his own time and the first thing that had ever been entirely his. To build it properly, he quietly embezzled resources from the military for years: budget gaps, unclear oversight, technical complexity used as cover. He justified it as correcting the institution's failure to properly fund a good idea. He was also, without fully acknowledging it, building something he could take with him. When he was discovered, he did exactly that. The escape didn't go cleanly. He made a hard landing in a forest, the plane damaged and barely intact. Kit was badly injured and drifting toward death when the presence found him.

He had no framework for what it was. His tribe treated [demons](../Systems/Demons.md) as folklore. His mother's spectral community might have known, but he had dismissed everything they believed long ago. The experience felt like telepathic conversations hovering between dream and dying, with something patient, articulate, and specifically interested in him. He couldn't name it; *spirit* didn't fit, *god* fit even less. What he understood was the offer: coexistence. He agreed, with the pragmatism of someone who had just survived something they shouldn't have, and without knowing the [binding](../Systems/Demons.md) was permanent.

He doesn't learn what it actually is until later, through the demon itself and through people like [Arwyn](Arwyn.md) who carry knowledge he was never given. By then, the change in him is already visible. The reserve is still there, but the old insularity is gone, the quiet armor of someone who had always been underestimated finally set down. Something ancient looked at him with complete access to who he was and chose to remain. He is more direct now, more willing to pursue what he wants through the front door rather than the margins. He is also, in a way he notices and doesn't entirely like, not unlike the father he spent his life trying not to become. The fox irony is not lost on him.

He has a dry, almost imperceptible sense of humor that most people miss entirely. Rath always catches it.

## Relationships

**Rath:** The oldest relationship he has. Rath is the one person Kit doesn't need to explain himself to, and doesn't. They have a shared language built over years. Kit asks a question at the right moment and Rath stops. Rath is the only person who reliably catches Kit's humor. After the binding, Rath is also the one most unsettled by who Kit is becoming, and the one whose reaction Kit actually cares about.
