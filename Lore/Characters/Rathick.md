# Rathick "Rath" Chee

**Species:** Cheetah\
**Variant:** None\
**Age:** 28\
**Height:** 180 cm\
**Weight:** 85 kg\
**Rank:** Sergeant\
**Specialist:** None

## Description

Rathick, known in service as Rath, is practical, direct, and action-oriented, relying on instinct and experience more than theory. Raised in the same isolated group as Kit, he learned discipline through physical training and repetition rather than formal instruction, giving him a strong sense of bodily awareness and situational judgment. Rath adapts quickly under pressure and prefers to solve problems through movement and decisiveness rather than discussion. He is loyal to a small, carefully chosen circle and remains skeptical of authority, following orders only when they align with his own sense of responsibility. In the military, Rath succeeds because he is consistent, reliable, and difficult to unsettle, earning trust through action rather than words.
