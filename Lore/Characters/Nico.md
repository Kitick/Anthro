# Nico Wyatt

**Species:** Red Panda\
**Age:** 21\
**Height:** 152 cm\
**Weight:** 56 kg\
**Role:** Student

## Overview

Nico is a 3rd year college student at (name) university, studying history and political science. He is inclined to be oblivious to his immediate surroundings, and has a tendency to get lost in thought. This sometimes causes others to think he is a bit inept, though he can be quite intelligent in his areas of interest. As he doesn't have many friends at school, he isn't very skilled with people or social interactions. He doesn't enjoy physical activity, so he is relatively thin and unalthletic. In his free time, he likes to collect retro clothing from thrift stores. His typical outfit consists of a brightly colored hoody, tennis shoes, and sweat pants. He can usually be found in the library at 2 am in a panic because the assignment he's working on is due in class at 8am. He has also taken a recent interest in ancient mythology and the occult, joining the college paranormal activity club. Tends to be a bit dramatic when describing, for example describing a minor fender bender as a bad car accident.

For his master's degree research, Nico has extensively studied a fictional nation similar to the Roman Empire. Through this research, he has become intimately familiar with historical patterns of military coups, specifically how militaries gradually consolidate power and influence before overthrowing civilian governments. When he begins to notice these same patterns emerging in his own country's current political landscape, he becomes convinced that the military is preparing for a takeover. This becomes the foundation for his term paper.

While researching current events to support his thesis, Nico reads a newspaper article about a protest conducted by a political opposition group. One of the key speakers makes a veiled reference to the very historical coup Nico has spent months studying. The reference is cryptic enough that most readers would miss it entirely, but to Nico it is unmistakable. However, the article frames the entire protest as violent rhetoric meant to incite a citizen revolt against the Senate. Nico realizes that the military has deliberately misrepresented the group's actual message: they were warning the Senate about the military's power grab, not calling for rebellion. When he attempts to reframe his paper to include this discovery, proving that the military is using disinformation to justify a crackdown on legitimate political opposition, he suddenly finds himself visited by an official from the military. After being kidnapped, he makes it his personal mission to discover more about the military, and uncovers more than he ever intended to find.
