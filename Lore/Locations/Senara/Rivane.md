# River City of Rivane

Rivane is one of the largest cities in Senara, with a unique geographical layout. A vast river divides the city into two parts, with a large bridge in the middle. At the center of the bridge is the shrine to the patron saint of the city and its' founding God, Vesrynn. Called the shrine of reflection, this sacred place is said to be so in tune with the river spirits that an aura of healing energy surrounds the premises. Many smaller shrines to individual river spirits dot the city, where citizens can burn incense or leave offerings as a form of prayer. It is believed that the river running through the city houses river spirits, and the herbs grown along the banks are of a higher quality/potency. Rivane is famous for its' high quality apothecaries and herbalists and offers training for such practices at the central cathedral. 

The people of Rivane practice a refined tradition of textile craftsmanship, viewing the creation and wearing of fine fabrics as a spiritual discipline that mirrors their herbalist heritage. Textiles are dyed using herbs cultivated along the river banks, their potency enhanced by the river spirits. Master dyers guard family techniques passed through generations, and the rarest dyes—derived from herbs most difficult to cultivate—are considered blessed by the spirits and reserved for ceremonial garments. Rather than chasing fleeting trends, the people of Rivane embrace seasonal harmony, adjusting their garments and color palettes to reflect the natural world's transitions. Each piece is worn and cared for across many years, with slight irregularities in weaving prized as evidence of human artistry and spirit. When fabrics become damaged or worn, they are transformed through careful repair or embellishment—tears are mended with contrasting threads or patches of complementary dyed cloth, and fading is celebrated as a natural evolution of the garment's beauty. These practices honor both the textile's history and the passage of time. It is believed that one's appearance —cultivated through intentional choices and respect for craft— reflects the depth of their inner spirit and dedication to refinement.
