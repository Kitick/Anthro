# Theocracy of Senara

## Modern Day

Modern day Senara is a theocratic collection of city states losely governed by the Vatican. It was created as a result of the Treaty of Tyrus, following a civil war between the ancient church and the newly formed senate. Culture of the nation consists of a reverence for spiritualism, ritualistic practices, and limited acceptance of technology. Most of the landscape is left in its' natural state, as extensive development and overpopulation is seen as detrimental to the spirits. Elemental and spiritual magic is a part of everyday life, and the purity of one's spirit is thought to be proportional to their magical capacity. It is believed that the spirit wanders from one's body after death, where it can be corrupted by impure emotions and desires. When this happens, a [demon](../../Systems/Demons.md) is created that can posses the bodies of living beings. They are thought to be evil monstrosities that corrupt pure spirits, and must be cleansed through ritualistic practices.

After the civil war, the Vatican declared that the 12 patron saints (who were the children of the 6 major dieties) would be the gaurdian saints of the 12 major cities in the newly refounded nation of Senara [Pantheon](Pantheon.md) The founding festival is an annual reinactment of this, declaring 1 person from each of the 12 cities as the saint's chosen to represent them in the festical.


## Historical Nation

The historical nation of Senara was a Theocratic Republic. Wary of the rising power of the senate, the Church attempted to restablish their control of the nation. They did this by garnering more power for the church, and dennouncing the government as heretical. The senate fought back by enacting more governmental policies that took power away from the church, like appointing governors to the cities who superceded the power of the local clergy. This led to the outbreak of a nasty civil war that lasted a little over a year. It ended with the Treaty of Tyrus, which established Senara as a separate nation governed by a theocracy and the church, and created the newly formed [Nation of Praxis](../Praxis/Praxis.md), dividing the nation.
