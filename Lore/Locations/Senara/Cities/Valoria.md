# Valoria

## Overview
Valoria is a medium-sized city among the twelve cities of Senara, celebrated as a beacon of honor, valor, and martial excellence. Founded and guided by the tenets of Saint Valoria, the city cultivates a philosophy that prizes righteousness, courage, and the virtues of direct, honorable combat. Valoria honors those who face their opponents with integrity and prove their worth through noble action and steadfast will.

## Patron Deity: Valoria of Honor

Valoria worships **Valoria, Saintess of Honor and Valor**, embodying righteous strength, truthfulness, and the noble character found in honorable struggle. Valoria represents the notion that true greatness comes not from deception or exploitation, but from meeting challenges head-on with courage and integrity. The people of Valoria view honor not as a weakness, but as the highest expression of strength—the confidence to win or lose fairly, with both dignity intact.

Valoria stands in eternal competition with their sibling deity Mealis, who values deception. This divine rivalry mirrors the cultural distinction between the two cities that worship them, creating a dynamic tension that has persisted for centuries.

## Founding Saint: Valoria of Honor and Valor

Saint Valoria founded the city bearing her name as a sanctuary for the brave, the honorable, and the righteously minded. Through her teachings, Saintess Valoria established that virtue in combat and in life flows from:

- The virtue of straightforward confrontation over cunning deception
- Unflinching courage in the face of any challenge
- Integrity as the measure of a warrior's true strength
- Honorable victory where the best and truest fighter wins

Saint Valoria's philosophy does not condemn tactical thinking, but rather insists that such tactics should never come at the expense of honor. A warrior who must resort to tricks has admitted their opponent's superiority of spirit.

## Cultural Values

The people of Valoria pride themselves on:

- Moral courage - The willingness to do what is right, regardless of difficulty
- Direct action - Confronting challenges head-on rather than seeking circumvention
- Personal honor - A reputation for truthfulness, fairness, and reliability
- Proving one's worth - Through demonstrating superior skill, strength, and righteous character in combat

## The Two Arenas

The tournament alternates between two distinct arenas, each designed to reflect the philosophies of its host city:

### The Arena of Shadows (Maelis)
Located in Maelis, this arena is intentionally designed to favor strategic thinking and adaptability:
- **Terrain and obstacles** - Multiple levels, pillars, and environmental features can be used tactically
- **Variable lighting** - Shadows and obscured sightlines create opportunities for misdirection
- **Limited visibility** - Combatants cannot see the entire arena from any single vantage point
- **Tactical environment** - The advantage goes to those who understand the terrain and exploit it creatively

This arena is where cunning and foresight shine. A skilled warrior who understands the space can overcome a stronger opponent through superior positioning and strategy.

### The Arena of Honor (Valoria)
Located in Valoria, this arena is designed to ensure fair, honorable combat:
- **Open and symmetrical** - Completely visible from all angles with no hidden advantages
- **Well-lit and clear** - No shadows or obscured sightlines; all combatants see equally
- **Multiple weapon choices** - Combatants may choose from a selection of honorably equivalent arms
- **Fairly delineated rules** - Clear boundaries and regulations that apply equally to all

This arena is where pure skill and bravery determine the victor. A warrior who fights with integrity and mastery can triumph against any opponent.

## The Monthly Tournament of Champions

The centerpiece of both cities' martial cultures is the **Monthly Tournament**, where the two cities test their philosophies against each other. This tournament serves multiple purposes:

### The Arena Rotation
- **Champion's choice** - The winner of each year's Annual Championship Match gets to select which arena will host the next year's tournaments
- **Strategic reward** - Champions typically choose their home city's arena, gaining a familiar advantage for the coming year
- **Prestige and power** - Winning is rewarded not just with glory, but with the power to shape the tournament's conditions
- **Rare exceptions** - Occasionally, a confident champion might choose the opposing city's arena to prove their philosophy's superiority

### Format
- **Monthly events** - Each month, Maelis and Valoria each send their finest warriors to compete
- **Combat duels** - Warriors face off in single combat, with victory determined by the arena's unique conditions
- **Arena advantage and challenge** - Champions must prove their philosophy works even when tested on unfamiliar ground

### Qualification Path
- **Monthly victories** qualify warriors as potential candidates
- **Annual Championship Match** - The year's greatest champion from Valoria faces the greatest from Maelis
- **The Prize** - The winning saint's city claims the annual title and prestige; the losing city must honor the victor's deity for the coming year

### Significance
This tournament is far more than entertainment—it is a theological and cultural battleground where both cities test their philosophies against each other. A Valoria victory suggests that honor and valor triumph even in a setting designed to favor trickery. A Maelis victory suggests that strategy and cunning are superior, even on neutral ground. Each year's hosting arena creates an additional layer of meaning: Valoria warriors fighting in the Arena of Shadows must prove their integrity transcends environmental advantage, while Maelis warriors in the Arena of Honor must demonstrate their worth without relying on terrain and tactical manipulation.

## The Praxis Connection

The military faction from Praxis occasionally attends the Monthly Tournament, though for purely practical purposes. Rather than celebrating the philosophical competition, Praxis operatives seek to:

- **Identify exceptional talent** - Both victories and close defeats reveal promising warriors
- **Recruit champions and runner-ups** - Those who prove themselves in the arena are valuable assets
- **Offer monetary compensation** - Praxis provides financial incentives for warriors willing to join their ranks

From Valoria's perspective, this is a form of validation—their warriors are so formidable that even the pragmatic militarists of Praxis covet them. From Praxis's perspective, Valoria produces some of the finest honorable combatants in Senara.

## Place in Senara

As one of the twelve cities, Valoria maintains its own laws, traditions, and governance while acknowledging Senara's greater authority. The city is a distinctive force in Senaran politics and culture—representing an aspirational philosophy of virtue and righteousness. Merchants and travelers come to Valoria not just for trade, but to witness combat that showcases the highest ideals of martial valor and personal honor.

## Key Institutions

- **The Gladiatorial Arena** - Center of monthly tournaments
- **The Cathedral of Saint Valoria** - Primary temple and spiritual center
- **The Hall of Champions** - Monument housing statues and records of all monthly tournament victors
- **The Warrior's Lodge** - Martial academy where warriors study the techniques and philosophies of honorable combat
