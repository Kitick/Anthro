# Valoria

## Overview
Valoria is a medium-sized city among the twelve cities of Senara, celebrated as a beacon of honor, valor, and martial excellence. Founded and guided by the tenets of Saint Valoria, the city cultivates a philosophy that prizes righteousness, courage, and the virtues of direct, honorable combat. Valoria honors those who face their opponents with integrity and prove their worth through noble action and steadfast will.

## Patron Deity: Valoria of Honor

Valoria worships **Valoria, Saintess of Honor and Valor**, embodying righteous strength, truthfulness, and the noble character found in honorable struggle. Valoria represents the notion that true greatness comes not from deception or exploitation, but from meeting challenges head-on with courage and integrity. The people of Valoria view honor not as a weakness, but as the highest expression of strength—the confidence to win or lose fairly, with both dignity intact.

Valoria stands in eternal competition with their sibling deity Mealis, who values deception. This divine rivalry mirrors the cultural distinction between the two cities that worship them, creating a dynamic tension that has persisted for centuries.

## Founding Saint: Valoria of Honor and Valor

Saint Valoria founded the city bearing her name as a sanctuary for the brave, the honorable, and the righteously minded. Through her teachings, Saintess Valoria established that virtue in combat and in life flows from:

- The virtue of straightforward confrontation over cunning deception
- Unflinching courage in the face of any challenge
- Integrity as the measure of a warrior's true strength
- Honorable victory where the best and truest fighter wins

Saint Valoria's philosophy does not condemn tactical thinking, but rather insists that such tactics should never come at the expense of honor. A warrior who must resort to tricks has admitted their opponent's superiority of spirit.

## Cultural Values

The people of Valoria pride themselves on:

- Moral courage - The willingness to do what is right, regardless of difficulty
- Direct action - Confronting challenges head-on rather than seeking circumvention
- Personal honor - A reputation for truthfulness, fairness, and reliability
- Proving one's worth - Through demonstrating superior skill, strength, and righteous character in combat

## Place in Senara

As one of the twelve cities, Valoria maintains its own laws, traditions, and governance while acknowledging Senara's greater authority. The city is a distinctive force in Senaran politics and culture—representing an aspirational philosophy of virtue and righteousness. Merchants and travelers come to Valoria not just for trade, but to witness combat that showcases the highest ideals of martial valor and personal honor.

## The Tournament

Valoria's martial culture finds its greatest expression in the shared [Monthly Tournament of Champions](../Tournament.md) with Maelis — a recurring contest that pits their philosophies directly against each other.

## Key Institutions

- **The Gladiatorial Arena** - Home of the Arena of Honor; see [Tournament of Champions](../Tournament.md)
- **The Cathedral of Saint Valoria** - Primary temple and spiritual center
- **The Hall of Champions** - Monument housing statues and records of all monthly tournament victors
- **The Warrior's Lodge** - Martial academy where warriors study the techniques and philosophies of honorable combat
