# Church of Senara Hierarchy

## Overview

The Church of Senara is a theological order created to spread the religion of (god) All ordained members progress through the same ranks, though specialization can occur at higher levels. Two main categories exist: those performing direct ministry and those in administrative or specialized roles. The Church recognizes both rank progression and specialist expertise.

## Clergy

- **Initiate**
  The entry level position within the church. This position typically includes acolytes who assist in maintaing the church and day to day operations, theological scholars who study divine texts, and low-rank healers who assist the sick and injured (through first aid and medicine, not healing magic)

- **Deacon**
  Deacons handle rituals, blessings, and tend to the spiritual needs of their communities (marriages, baptism, blessings). They make up the majority of the Church's presence among the population.

- **Priest**
  First ordained rank, these are the base clergy and staff conducting daily ministry and services. Within the Church hierarchy, they oversee spiritual discipline and address serious matters of faith and doctrine with the public.

## Leadership

- **Bishop**

  This is the first command rank and serves as the practical decision-maker for their assigned region or diocese. Bishops oversee priests and deacons, organize local services and rituals, and ensure the Pope's doctrine is maintained. They act as intermediaries between the Pope's will and local communities.

- **Cardinal**

  These act as major area leaders and are responsible for high-level decisions within their assigned discipline or region. Cardinals organize missions and assignments, oversee bishops, and advise the Pope on matters of governance and faith. A Cardinal is the highest internally promoted rank within the Church.

## Supreme Authority

- **Pope**

  The singular supreme leader of the Church and theocratic government, responsible for final decisions on all matters of faith, doctrine, and governance. The Pope interprets divine will, sets Church direction, and interfaces with secular authorities. The Pope is chosen by Cardinals in conclave and holds authority until death or abdication.

- **Inquisitor General (Specialized Cardinal-level)**

  Chief enforcer of doctrinal purity and investigator of heresy and demonic corruption across all Church territories. The Inquisitor General commands a network of purifiers and inquisitors, conducting interrogations and ritualistic cleansing. This role operates at the same authority level as Cardinals but with specialized focus on spiritual threats.
