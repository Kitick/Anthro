## Setup

Arwyn, the local herbalist in the western town of Rivane, starts every day preparing remedies from her list of orders for the villagers. Once she finishes a few different concoctions (brewing tutorial?) She goes to her friend [witch]'s hut, where they take a break to have tea. After a brief conversation, [witch] tells her that she drew a bad omen during her daily tarot (or other equivalent) reading. Both friends are uncertain about the future with this ill omen.

Afterwards, Arwyn departs to the nearby forest to gather required herbs for her shop. While there, she notices what appears to be a group of men lost in the woods. She quickly realizes that they are armed, and wearing the emblem of the national government. Her village is not on friendly terms with the government, so she is immediately on alert at their presence so close to her home.

## Flow / Beats

1. Morning: Arwyn prepares remedies and visits the witch's hut for tea and a reading.
2. The witch mentions drawing a bad omen and both friends express concern.
3. Arwyn goes to the forest to gather herbs and encounters armed men with the national government's emblem.

## Choices / Player Options

- **Talk / Deception:** Talk to the soldiers and tell them the wrong way to reach the village (persuasion/deception)
- **Alert / Quick:** Quickly rush back to tell the village elder (active, stealth?)
- **Aggressive / Attack:** (aerosol chloroform attack)

## Outcomes & Consequences

- Soldiers misdirected, village remains safe for the moment
- Elder alerted, village prepares defenses or negotiates
- Attack escalates conflict and may draw government attention
